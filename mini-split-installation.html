Ductless mini splits

Mini split installation for Bucks and Montgomery County

A ductless mini split is a heat pump. An outdoor unit sits outside. One or more indoor heads mount on a wall inside. A refrigerant line set connects them through a small opening. There is no ductwork. Each indoor head is its own zone, with its own temperature, and it both heats and cools.

Ice Peak Heating & Cooling installs, repairs, and tunes up ductless mini splits from the shop at 701 E Pennsylvania Blvd in Feasterville-Trevose. The same crew handles central AC, furnaces, heat pumps, and full system replacements.

Single zone and multi-zone

A single-zone system is one indoor head and one outdoor unit. That is the job behind the $3,499 starting price: a new ductless install for one zone. It fits a room the central system never reached, an addition, a finished space over a garage, or a top floor that overheats.

A multi-zone system uses one outdoor unit and several indoor heads, so different rooms can be set separately. Use it when more than one room needs its own temperature and you do not want a separate outdoor unit for each. There is no published multi-zone price. The free in-home estimate is where that number comes from. The one-zone starting price does not scale by simply multiplying rooms.

Heating and cooling from the same unit

Because a mini split is a heat pump, it cools in summer and heats in winter. In a house that already has a furnace, the mini split can take one zone while the furnace keeps the rest. In a house with no ducts, the heads can be the heating and the cooling. Ice Peak also offers rebate guidance for qualifying heat pump upgrades. This page does not state a rebate amount. Programs and equipment decide that.
