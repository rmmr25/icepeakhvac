Heating and cooling in Feasterville-Trevose

Feasterville-Trevose is home base. The shop is at 701 E Pennsylvania Blvd, on the Pennsylvania Boulevard stretch of Street Road (PA-132), in ZIP 19053. Feasterville runs along that commercial corridor. Trevose sits toward the SEPTA West Trenton line. The houses between them are mostly mid-century twins, ranches, and split-levels, with townhouse pockets mixed in.

A lot of those houses still heat with a basement furnace and cool with a condenser on a narrow side yard. Split-levels often have one thermostat and an upstairs that runs warmer than the living room. When a finished room over the garage was added later, the original ducts usually do not reach it.

Because the shop is on Pennsylvania Blvd, Feasterville-Trevose is the shortest trip on the schedule. Same-day appointments are offered, and the phone is answered 24/7.

What we do on these streets

Summer calls are usually a central AC that quit, a condenser that is icing, or an upstairs that never catches up. Winter calls are furnace repairs and safety inspections. When both the furnace and the outdoor unit are due, a matched replacement is the job the homepage prices from $6,499 for a typical home. Size and the existing setup change the final number, which is what the free estimate is for.

Heat pumps are the other path when you want one system for heating and cooling. Indoor air quality work, including duct cleaning and sealing, is on the same truck when the ducts themselves are the problem.

  • Furnace repair, replacement, and safety inspections

Mini splits for the room the ducts miss

A garage-over room or a later addition along this corridor is a common reason to skip extending old ductwork. Ductless mini split installation puts one indoor head in that room and an outdoor heat pump outside. It heats and cools. A new one-zone mini split install starts at $3,499. That is the figure already listed on the homepage for a typical install. The final price comes after a free in-home estimate.

Multi-zone equipment, for more than one room, is quoted on the same visit. There is no published multi-zone price. Repair and tune-ups for a mini split you already own are part of the same service.
