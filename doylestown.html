Home · Doylestown

Bucks County

HVAC in Doylestown, the Bucks County seat

Doylestown is the farthest of these core towns from the Feasterville-Trevose shop, and it has the oldest housing in the borough. Around the courthouse, many homes date from before central air was standard. Walls are plaster. Some houses still heat with a boiler. Adding a full duct system means opening finished rooms, which is why a ductless heat pump is often the practical way to cool them.

Doylestown Township is a different map. Larger lots toward the hospital and out Ferry Road hold newer colonials that already have furnaces and central air. Those visits look like the rest of central Bucks: repair, tune-up, or a planned replacement.

Ice Peak schedules Doylestown from the shop at 701 E Pennsylvania Blvd. There is no second location up here.

Older borough houses

If the house has no ducts, a mini split heat pump can heat and cool the rooms you pick without a new duct system. One head is a single zone. Several heads on one outdoor unit are a multi-zone system, quoted after someone has seen the rooms and the electrical setup.

If the house has a boiler, boiler service is separate from the mini split. The homepage already lists gas furnace and boiler installation, repair, and safety inspections. A mini split does not replace that inspection when the boiler is still the heat for the rest of the house.

Ducted township houses

For houses that already have a furnace and central AC, repair and replacement are the main work. A matched full system starts at $6,499 for a typical home. Final price after a free in-home estimate. Heat pump conversions are available, including rebate guidance when an upgrade qualifies.

  • Furnace, boiler, and AC service for houses that already have them
  • Full system replacement starting at $6,499 for a typical home
